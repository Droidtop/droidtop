### Changed
- Notifications on the companion show each app's icon, the title and one short line, with the rest behind More,
  instead of the app's name glued to every title and its whole technical text.
- Things apps keep running (a sync, a VPN, charging, wireless debugging) are no longer listed as messages: a sync or
  download with progress shows its app's icon and ring in the status line, and the rest is folded under "Running in
  the background". Charging, wireless debugging and VPN notices are left out, since droidtop already shows them.
- Clear all says how many it will clear, asks first, and only appears when something can be cleared. The folded
  Notifications heading shows the count and the apps' icons.
- Apps' "Tap to ..." hints are left out everywhere droidtop lists notifications.
