### Added
- Hacker Keyboard Reborn moves: a pressed key dips and springs back, the key preview and the long-press popup grow out
  of the key, the emoji, kaomoji, clipboard and macro panels slide in over the keys, and new suggestions fade in. With
  Android's "Remove animations" on, or animations turned off in developer options, nothing moves.
- Each kind of key has its own vibration: letters, modifiers (Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Fn), Delete, and Space and Enter all
  feel different. The keyboard droidtop shows in its own screens follows Android's touch feedback setting.

### Changed
- "Vibrate on keypress" is now "Vibration strength": Off, Light, Medium, Strong, or Custom, which buzzes every key for
  your "Vibrate duration" as before. Your old choice carries over, duration included.
