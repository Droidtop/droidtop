### Added
- Hacker Keyboard Reborn's clipboard panel is new: clips are grouped into Pinned, Recent and Earlier, each shows two
  lines of text, how long ago it was copied and a pin mark, and a search finds clips as you type on the keyboard.
  Hold a clip to paste, pin, unpin or delete it; with a controller, X opens the same menu, A pastes and B closes.
- A "Keep clips for" setting (1 hour, 1 day, 1 week or Until cleared) removes older clips; pinned clips always stay.
  It starts at Until cleared, so your history is kept as before until you choose.
- "Clear unpinned" in the clipboard panel asks before it clears, and pinned clips stay.

### Fixed
- A copy made in a password field is no longer kept in the clipboard history when a hardware keyboard is attached or
  the keys are on the add-on screen.
- Text droidtop types into other apps through the clipboard is no longer kept in the clipboard history as if you had
  copied it.
