### Added
- Chinese typing on droidtop's keyboard. Pick Chinese (中文, mainland or Taiwan) in the keyboard's languages and type
  pinyin on the usual layout: "nihao" offers 你好 and more to pick, and space takes the first. Picking a word for part
  of what you typed keeps the rest to pick next. A button on the strip switches between 简 (simplified), 繁
  (traditional) and plain letters.
- The first time you type Chinese, the keyboard downloads its dictionary (7 MB, 15 MB once unpacked) and says how far
  it has come. The keyboard's settings have a Chinese row to forget what it learned from your typing or to delete the
  dictionary.
