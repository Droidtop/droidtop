### Changed
- A PC game's page has six tabs, always shown even when one has nothing in it: Overview, Versions, Activity, Extras,
  Details and Show thread. Activity holds when you last played, your play time, how many times you played and the
  game's achievements. Show thread opens the game's linked source page.
- Beside Play the page now has Edit metadata, which holds Scrape and the game's title, and Options, which holds Saves,
  Sign in for achievements, Choose match, View media, Collections and the rest of the game's menu. Both open next to
  their button and work by touch, mouse and controller.
- A row of small status icons under the game's name shows at a glance whether it is ready to play, has an update, has
  its cloud saves synced, has saves found, has achievements, or still needs scraping.
