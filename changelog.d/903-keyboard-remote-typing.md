### Changed
- While a phone types into this device through Type from another device, the keyboard shows it in a row of its own
  above the tools: "Phone connected · typing" and what was last typed. In a password field the text is not shown, and
  when something did not arrive the row says "Not received" and why, until the next thing lands.
- Text a phone or a KDE Connect device sends to this device's clipboard is now also on the keyboard's clipboard panel.
  Nothing is kept while incognito, with clipboard history off, or while you are in a password field.
