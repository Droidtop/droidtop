### Added
- Japanese typing on droidtop's keyboard. Pick Japanese in the keyboard's languages and type romaji on the usual
  layout: "nihongo" becomes にほんご, and the strip offers 日本語 and more to pick. Space converts, the arrows move
  between parts of a sentence (with Shift to make a part shorter or longer), and after a word the strip suggests what
  may come next. A button on the strip switches between hiragana, katakana and plain letters.
- The first time you type Japanese, the keyboard downloads its 19 MB dictionary and says how far it has come. The
  keyboard's settings have a Japanese row to forget what it learned from your typing or to delete the dictionary.
