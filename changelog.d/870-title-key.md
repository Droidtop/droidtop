### Changed
- A game is now the same game on every device: the handheld and your computers name a game by one key, its title in
  Lutris's form ("Being a DIK" is being-a-dik), so a game merged on one device is merged on the others. Computers that
  have not updated yet are still understood. Two folders whose names differ only in spacing joined in lower case
  ("ThiefofHearts" and "Thief of Hearts") are now two games; use "The same game as..." to join them.
