### Changed
- A console game's page now looks and works like a PC game's: the same art and status icons at the top, Play, Edit
  metadata, Options and the favourite star, and the same six tabs. Scrape is the first row of Edit metadata, next to
  the full metadata editor; Saves, Sign in for achievements, Choose match, View media and Collections are in Options,
  and the game menu button opens Options too.
- On a console game's page, Play says which emulator runs the game, or that none is installed for its system. Its
  play history and achievements are on Activity, a Switch game's update and DLC on Versions, its manual, video and
  your own "open with" apps on Extras, and its system, emulator and file on Details.
- An Android app's page from the Apps tab uses the same page, with Open, App info and Uninstall.
