### Added
- Settings > Appearance > Colour source: droidtop can follow your device's own colours (Android 12 and later, from
  your wallpaper), your Gaming theme's colours, or a custom droidtop theme. Device colours is the new default where
  the device has them.
- Tint themes to my colours, off by default, gives your Gaming theme your accent colour and leaves the rest of the
  theme as it is.
