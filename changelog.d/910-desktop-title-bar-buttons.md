### Added
- Linux windows on the desktop now have close, minimise and maximise buttons at the right end of their title bars,
  sized for a finger and working with a mouse too. Maximise turns into restore once a window is maximised.
- A Linux window can be resized by pressing near any edge or corner, in a band about a fingertip wide, instead of
  hitting its thin border exactly. A window in fullscreen shows no buttons.
