### Added
- Containers shows each container's size beside its state. It says "Measuring…" the first time and is remembered
  until the container changes, so opening the list again is quick.
- Containers has a Downloaded images section: turn off keeping downloaded images, choose how much space they may take
  (2 GB, 5 GB, 10 GB or no limit), and clear them. Clearing never touches your containers.

### Changed
- When a container's size can't be read in full, droidtop now shows no size instead of a smaller, wrong one.
