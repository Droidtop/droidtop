### Changed
- An Android app's page now speaks of the app, not a game: its empty tabs, its status icons and its menus say "app",
  and the controller's options button is named App options.
- An app's page shows what droidtop knows of it: its installed version and, when one of your app sources has a newer
  one, an Update row that installs it; when you last opened it; and where it came from, when it was installed and
  last updated, and how many permissions it asks for.
- An app's Options now also has Hide from droidtop and Mark as game, as in the Apps tab's menu. Uninstall is only
  offered for apps Android lets you remove.

### Fixed
- An app hidden from the Apps tab's menu no longer shows up again when you open an app's page or another tab and
  come back.
