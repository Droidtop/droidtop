### Added
- Hacker Keyboard Reborn has new themes: Dynamic (wallpaper), which takes its colours from your wallpaper on Android 12
  and later, and High contrast. In droidtop, Match droidtop gives the keyboard the colours droidtop's screens use.
- The theme setting shows a small preview of each theme's keys, so you can see a theme before picking it. Every classic
  Hacker's Keyboard theme is still there.
- A "Light and dark" setting for the modern themes: follow the system (in droidtop, droidtop's own Light or dark
  setting), always light, or always dark.

### Changed
- "Follow light or dark" is now called Modern. Key labels on every modern theme, and on the keyboard droidtop shows in
  its own screens, are always clear against their keys, whatever the colours.
- The keyboard droidtop shows in its own screens is light or dark with droidtop, even when a classic theme is picked
  for the keyboard elsewhere.

### Fixed
- Hacker's Material Light and Material Black themes had each other's names in the theme list.
